# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report vulnerabilities **privately** by email to
**security@tillisoftware.com**. Do not open a public issue, pull request or
discussion.

Include what you can: affected crate and version or commit, a description of
the issue, a minimal reproducer (for example an input that makes a decoder
panic or read out of bounds), and its impact as you understand it. Never send
real cardholder or account data; synthetic inputs are enough.

We will acknowledge your report within three business days and keep you
informed as we investigate.

## Coordinated disclosure

We follow a **90-day** coordinated disclosure window, counted from the day we
acknowledge the report. We aim to release a fix well before then, will agree
the disclosure date with you, and will credit you in the advisory unless you
prefer otherwise. Fixed vulnerabilities are published as GitHub security
advisories and reported to the [RustSec advisory database](https://rustsec.org/).
